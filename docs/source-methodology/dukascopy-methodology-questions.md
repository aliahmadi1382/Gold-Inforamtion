# Draft: historical gold export permissions and methodology

Prepared for the project owner; not sent. Do not attach credentials, downloaded price tables, private reports or account information.

Subject: XAU/USD historical exports — local noncommercial research permissions and methodology

We are evaluating the official Historical Data Export widget for personal, local, noncommercial gold research. Before any further acquisition or database integration, could you clarify:

1. Is there a specific historical-data licence that permits retaining exported XAU/USD CSV data in a local SQLite database, computing descriptive statistics and displaying results privately on localhost? The general website terms restrict database construction, while the export tool enables downloads. Which terms govern this use?
2. Is automated acquisition permitted through a documented interface? If written consent is required, what is the approved process? We will not enable an automated adapter without this clarification.
3. Are XAU/USD historical BID/ASK OHLC prices quoted in USD per troy ounce throughout the history? Are there changes in units, precision, instrument definition or methodology, with effective dates?
4. For the widget's one-hour Etc/UTC exports, are timestamps bar starts and closes the last observed quote within each hour? How are empty hours, flat bars and partial bars handled?
5. Where can we obtain historical trading sessions, holiday exceptions and schedule changes with effective dates? Current schedules alone cannot certify earlier years.
6. Can historical values be corrected? Is there a revision archive or a documented time when a closed bar first becomes available?

Public documentation links would be useful. No account opening, paid subscription, redistribution or model training is requested.
